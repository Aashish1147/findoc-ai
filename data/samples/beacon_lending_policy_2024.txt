================================================================================
BEACON COMMERCIAL LENDING LTD. - UNDERWRITING POLICY & TERMS (2024)
================================================================================

SECTION 1: UNDERWRITING STANDARDS AND CREDIT CRITERIA
Beacon Commercial Lending Ltd. originates secured commercial real estate and
equipment loans across selected business sectors. Effective October 1, 2024,
all credit facilities are governed by the following underwriting thresholds:
- Minimum Borrower Credit Score: 680 FICO score for principal guarantors.
  Guarantors with credit scores below 680 require secondary board approval.
- Maximum Debt-to-Income (DTI) Ratio: 43.0% calculated on trailing twelve-month
  verified cash flows.
- Maximum Loan-to-Value (LTV) Ratio: 80.0% for stabilized owner-occupied
  commercial real estate; 75.0% for non-owner occupied investment properties.
- Minimum Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR): 1.25x under primary stress scenarios.

SECTION 2: LOAN PRICING, ORIGINATION FEES, AND PREPAYMENT TERMS
Beacon structures pricing according to risk tier and facility duration:
- Standard Loan Origination Fee: 1.50% of gross loan commitment amount, payable
  at settlement.
- Loan Documentation Fee: $750.00 flat fee charged at loan closing.
- Environmental Assessment Review Fee: $1,200.00 where applicable.
- Prepayment Penalty Structure:
  * Repayment within Year 1 (Months 1-12): 2.0% penalty of outstanding principal.
  * Repayment within Year 2 (Months 13-24): 1.0% penalty of outstanding principal.
  * Repayment after 24 Months: 0.0% penalty (no prepayment penalty applies after
    the completion of month 24).

SECTION 3: FACILITY SIZES AND AMORTIZATION GUIDELINES
Beacon provides debt capital within structured boundaries:
- Minimum Commercial Facility: $250,000.00.
- Maximum Single-Borrower Facility: $15,000,000.00.
- Maximum Amortization Period: 25 years with a mandatory 10-year balloon maturity
  or refinancing checkpoint.
- Interest Rate Basis: Floating rate indexed to 30-Day Average SOFR plus a margin
  ranging from 2.75% to 4.50% depending on risk tier.

SECTION 4: PROHIBITED COLLATERAL AND LENDING RESTRICTIONS
To preserve asset quality and comply with internal credit charters, Beacon
Commercial Lending strictly prohibits lending against the following assets:
- Speculative undeveloped raw land lacking municipal zoning or utilities.
- Cannabis-related enterprises, dispensaries, or cultivation facilities.
- Single-purpose entertainment or casino gaming establishments.
- Residential multi-family properties exceeding 4 units (covered by separate
  multi-family affiliate).

SECTION 5: NOTICES AND REGULATORY DISCLAIMER
Beacon Commercial Lending is an equal opportunity commercial lender. All terms
and rate commitments are subject to credit committee ratification and formal
closing documentation.
