================================================================================
APEX FINANCIAL GROUP INC. - ANNUAL FINANCIAL REPORT & DISCLOSURES (FY2024)
================================================================================

SECTION 1: CORPORATE PROFILE AND EXECUTIVE LEADERSHIP
Apex Financial Group Inc. is a diversified financial services holding company
headquartered at 450 Lexington Avenue, New York, NY 10017. Founded in 2012, the
company provides commercial banking, treasury management, and retail wealth
solutions across North America. As of December 31, 2024, the institution manages
approximately $4.85 billion in consolidated assets under management.

The executive management team is headed by Sarah Jenkins, who has served as
President and Chief Executive Officer (CEO) since March 2019. The Chief Financial
Officer (CFO) is Marcus Vance, CPA, and the Chief Risk Officer (CRO) is Elena
Rostova. The board of directors consists of nine members, seven of whom qualify
as independent directors under standard corporate governance definitions.

SECTION 2: FY2024 FINANCIAL PERFORMANCE AND OPERATING METRICS
For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, Apex Financial Group achieved record
operating performance:
- Total Net Revenue: $145.2 million, representing a 14.2% increase compared to
  the previous fiscal year ($127.1 million in FY2023).
- Net Income: $32.4 million, up from $26.8 million in FY2023.
- Operating Margin: 22.3%, an expansion of 180 basis points year-over-year.
- Research and Technology Spend: $18.5 million dedicated to digital core banking.
- Diluted Earnings Per Share (EPS): $2.14 per share based on 15.1 million weighted
  average common shares outstanding.

Return on average tangible common equity (ROATCE) was 14.6%, and the company
maintained an efficiency ratio of 58.4%.

SECTION 3: ACCOUNT FEE SCHEDULE AND ADMINISTRATIVE CHARGES
Apex Financial Group adheres to transparent retail and commercial pricing:
- Monthly Account Maintenance Fee: $15.00 per month.
  Condition: The monthly maintenance fee is completely waived if the client
  maintains a minimum average daily balance exceeding $5,000.00 across eligible
  deposit accounts during the monthly statement cycle.
- Domestic Outbound Wire Transfer Fee: $25.00 per transaction.
- International Outbound Wire Transfer Fee: $45.00 per transaction.
- Inbound Wire Transfers: $0.00 (no charge for either domestic or foreign inbound).
- Overdraft Fee: $35.00 per incident, subject to a daily maximum cap of three (3)
  overdraft fees per account ($105.00 maximum daily fee).
- Stop Payment Order: $30.00 per request, valid for a 6-month operational window.

SECTION 4: CAPITAL ADEQUACY AND REGULATORY RISK FACTORS
Apex Financial Group maintains capital ratios well above standard Basel III
regulatory requirements:
- Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1) Capital Ratio: 12.8%, compared to the regulatory
  minimum requirement of 10.5%.
- Tier 1 Risk-Based Capital Ratio: 14.2%.
- Total Capital Ratio: 16.1%.
- Interest Rate Sensitivity: Internal modeling indicates that an instantaneous
  parallel upward shift of +100 basis points (+1.00%) in benchmark interest rates
  would decrease net interest margin by $3.2 million on an annualized basis.
- Commercial Real Estate Exposure: Loans secured by non-owner occupied commercial
  real estate represent 18.4% of the total loan portfolio.
